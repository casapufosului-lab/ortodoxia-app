## Verification Notes

1. All prayers are traditional Orthodox texts passed down through centuries
2. Variants of the Lord's Prayer (Tatăl nostru) follow the Orthodox Slavonic tradition with Romanian vernacular adaptation
3. The Jesus Prayer (Rugăciunea lui Iisus) follows the traditional Orthodox formula
4. The Trisagion (Sfinte Dumnezeule) is the standard Orthodox hymn
5. The Cherubic Hymn (Cuvine-se cu adevărat) follows the traditional text
6. The Holy Trinity prayer (Preasfântă Treime) is a traditional Orthodox prayer of invocation
7. The Glory (Slavă Tatălui) follows the standard Orthodox doxology
8. The Annunciation (Născătoare de Dumnezeu) follows the traditional Orthodox hymn
9. The Akathist to the Theotokos (Sub milostivirea ta) follows the traditional Orthodox akathist text
10. The Prayer of St. Basil (Rugăciunea vameșului) is the traditional morning prayer attributed to St. Basil the Great
